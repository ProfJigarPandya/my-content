Network Topology Hadoop Cluster - Rack Awareness

Learn about rack awareness from here 
https://hadoop.apache.org/docs/stable/hadoop-project-dist/hadoop-common/RackAwareness.html

Here, in our computer engineering department, each labs are considered/architect as a rack. Rack awareness architecture is enabled using following configurations and the script attached herewith.
 
core-site.xml        <name>net.topology.script.file.name</name>
        <value>/opt/hadoop/etc/hadoop/scripts/topology.sh</value>
